Your router is probably out of date. Here is how to check

A few minutes of maintenance can make your home internet faster and safer.

irtBy Irtnews staff24 November 2026 · 4 min readShare on X

The router is often the most forgotten device in the home. Yet it handles every website, video call and smart device you use. Keeping it updated can improve speed and protect you from security problems.

Check for updates

Most routers can be managed through a web page or an app from your internet provider. Look for a section called firmware or software update. Many newer routers update automatically, but older ones may need you to start the update yourself.

Change the default password

If your router still uses the password printed on the sticker for its admin page, change it to something unique. This stops anyone on your network from changing your settings.

If your router is more than five years old, it may no longer get updates at all.

Think about placement

Put the router somewhere central and raised, away from thick walls, metal objects and fish tanks. A better position can improve speed more than paying for a faster plan.

If your provider has stopped supporting your model, ask whether they will replace it. Many will do so free of charge.
