Corner shops turn to instant payments as card fees bite

Owners say paying straight from a bank account to the shop saves them money each month.

irtBy Irtnews staff24 November 2026 · 4 min read

Small shop owners say card fees have become one of their biggest costs after rent and staff. A growing number are now offering customers another option: paying directly from their bank account by scanning a code with their phone.

These instant payments move money from the customer's account to the shop's account in seconds. Because they skip the card networks, fees are usually lower.

How customers pay

At the till, the shop shows a code on a small screen or printed card. The customer scans it with their banking app, checks the amount and approves the payment. The shop sees the money arrive almost immediately.

On a busy day, every small fee adds up.

Not for everyone yet

Some customers are wary of using a new method, and not every bank app supports it smoothly. Shop owners say they will keep accepting cards, but hope more people will try the cheaper option.

Industry groups are calling for clearer rules so that customers have the same protection as card payments if something goes wrong.
