Cup draw pairs two old rivals in the next round

The tie will be played in January, with tickets expected to sell out fast.

irtBy Irtnews staff24 November 2026 · 2 min readShare on X

The cup draw has thrown up the tie everyone wanted: two old rivals will meet in the next round in January.

The clubs have not met in the competition for several years, and both have strong records in cup football. Supporters are already planning travel.

Cup ties like this are why the competition still matters.

Ticket details will be announced by both clubs in the coming days. Fans are advised to buy only through official club channels.
