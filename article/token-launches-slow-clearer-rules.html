New token launches slow as traders wait for clearer rules

Builders say they are holding back projects until regulators set out what is allowed.

irtBy Irtnews staff24 November 2026 · 3 min read

The number of new tokens being launched has slowed in recent weeks, as builders and traders wait for clearer rules from regulators in several major markets.

Developers say uncertainty over how tokens will be classified, and which disclosures they will need to make, has made some teams cautious about launching.

Quality over quantity

Some in the industry welcome the slowdown, arguing that fewer rushed launches could mean fewer projects that collapse soon after trading begins.

A quieter market is not always a weaker one.

Traders are being urged to research any new token carefully: check who controls the supply, whether the code has been reviewed and how easy it is to sell. This article is general information, not financial advice.
