The race to build a phone battery that lasts a week

Labs are testing new materials that pack more charge into the same space.

irtBy Irtnews staff24 November 2026 · 4 min read

Phones have become faster, sharper and smarter, but most still need charging every day. Battery researchers are trying to change that.

The goal is to pack more energy into the same space without making batteries heavier, hotter or less safe. Several labs are testing new materials for the parts of the battery that store charge.

The options

One approach adds silicon to the battery's negative side, which can hold more charge than the graphite used today. Another replaces the liquid inside the battery with a solid material, which could be safer and store more energy.

The hard part is not making a better battery once. It is making millions of them cheaply.

When will we see it

Some improvements are already arriving in new phones. A true week-long battery is still some years away. In the meantime, software that manages power more cleverly is doing a lot of the work.

Simple habits also help: lowering screen brightness, turning off features you do not use and avoiding leaving your phone in very hot places.
